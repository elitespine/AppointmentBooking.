About the Doctor

Healing Through Movement & Compassion

At Elite Spine Physiotherapy Clinic, we believe physiotherapy is more than just treating pain, it’s about understanding your body, restoring movement and helping you get back to the life you love.

Dr. Krupali Sawant (PT) takes a personalised, patient-first approach to every treatment, combining clinical expertise, hands on therapy and targeted rehabilitation to create care that is tailored to you.

Your pain has a story. We help you understand it, treat it, and move forward.
